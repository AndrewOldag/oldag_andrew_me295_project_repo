## 2. Remedies and settings (`agents/actions.py`, carried out by `measure/remedies.py`)
| Remedy | Settings | How it is carried out | Set by |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| `register_frames` | none | ECC Euclidean registration, frame to frame, on a band from where the record's centre REGR falls under 5 % of its peak to the basal end of the midline, 25 px inside both frames | decisions 2026-10-11 |
| `change_frame_stride` | half, double | analysis frame stride | decisions 2026-10-10 |
| `smooth_midline` | light (sigma 10 px along s, 1 frame in time) | time smoothing in world coordinates, fitted-line end padding (3 frames) | decisions 2026-10-11 (11c); `strong` withdrawn 2026-10-11, confirmed by Andrew 2026-10-12 and rejected in `measure/remedies.py` |
| `crop_or_mask` | crop_to_main_root, mask_second_root | band of 45 px around the midline source | decisions 2026-10-10 |
| `enlarge_patch` | 1.5x, 2x | tracker disk radius 22 or 30 px (base 15) | decisions 2026-10-10 |
| `use_width_free_ratio` | none | no re-run; report centre strain rate and width-free ratio; plane sections skipped | Andrew, 2026-10-10 |

- Roll back: a remedy that makes no failing check pass and lowers none to 3/4 of its value is undone; the step counts. A check skipped on purpose (plane sections under the width-free ratio) counts as resolved; a check that can no longer be computed does not
- Remedy result: `settings_left: "no further setting: ..."` when no setting of the remedy can be applied to the current settings (P3)
