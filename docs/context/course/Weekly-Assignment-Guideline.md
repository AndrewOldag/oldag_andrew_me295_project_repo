# Weekly Assignment: Read a Research Paper with AI

# Goal

Use AI to understand a research paper that interests you. Show how you guide its responses, check them against the paper, and improve your own understanding.

# What to do

Choose a research paper related to your interests and a question that helps your research, such as understanding a method, interpreting a figure, or checking a derivation. Your question does not need to match the week’s topic.

Before using AI, read the relevant part yourself. In 2–3 sentences, record what you think it means and what you do not yet understand. Keep this original note so you can compare it with your understanding afterward.

Start with an AI model. As the course progresses, use your own single-agent or multi-agent system and apply the methods you have learned.

Complete at least three meaningful guidance rounds after the first response. One round (or “hop”) means one guidance message from you and one response from the model or agent system.

In each round, address a specific gap and check the new response. You could ask AI to clarify an equation’s terms, check whether a figure supports a claim, or explain whether a method’s assumptions fit your research. Choose guidance based on the response; these are options, not a fixed script. Repeating “try again” or only changing the format does not count.

If the first answer is useful, explore it more deeply or check its claims against the paper. Do not invent errors to fill three rounds. Keep an honest record, including what remains unresolved.
